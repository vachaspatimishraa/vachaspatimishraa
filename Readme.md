### 💡 Engineering Principles

> *"Code is written once, but read, maintained, and audited thousands of times. Keep your domain pure, your dependencies minimal, and your UI at 60 FPS."*

- **Strict YAGNI**: No speculative abstractions; build what solves real architectural requirements.
- **Offline-First by Design**: Software should never display a loading spinner for previously cached local data.
- **Immutable State Trees**: Unidirectional data flow eliminates race conditions and ensures predictable UI states.
